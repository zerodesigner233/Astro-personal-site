This series explores the boundaries between natural landscapes and digital aesthetics.

## Inspiration

Each piece in this series starts with a photograph of a real location, which is then abstracted through a process of color reduction and geometric simplification.

## Process

- Reference photography
- Color palette extraction
- Geometric decomposition
- Digital painting in Procreate
- Final compositing in Photoshop

## Tools Used

- Procreate (iPad Pro)
- Adobe Photoshop
- Custom brush packs

A collection of album cover designs created for independent electronic music artists.

## Design Philosophy

Each cover is designed to visually represent the sonic character of the album, using bold geometry and limited color palettes.

## Clients

- Neon Drift Records
- Pixel Sound Collective
- Ambient Works Label

## Process

1. Listen to the full album
2. Mood board creation
3. Concept sketches (3 directions)
4. Digital execution
5. Final delivery in multiple formats
